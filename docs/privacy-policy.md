# DrumFord X — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-05-29_

DrumFord X is a drum chart visualizer published by **Fordnet**. This policy
explains what the app does and does not do with your data. Short version: **we
don't collect anything.**

## What we collect

**Nothing.** DrumFord X does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal
or personally identifiable information. There are no user accounts, no logins,
no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash/usage tracking, and no third-party
trackers of any kind.

## Data stored on your device

DrumFord X stores the following **locally on your device only** (in the app's
private storage / IndexedDB). It never leaves your device:

- Drum charts you import or download, and their associated audio and cover art
- Your kit customization settings (lane order, colors, tuning) and per-app preferences

You can delete this data at any time by removing songs in the app or
uninstalling DrumFord X.

## Network connections

DrumFord X makes network requests only to **paradb.net**, a community-run
database of drum charts, and only when you use the in-app "Community Charts"
feature:

- To fetch the public catalog of charts (so you can browse and search)
- To download a chart you choose to download

These requests do not include any personal information about you — they're
ordinary public API calls. DrumFord X does not send paradb.net (or anyone else)
any identifying data.

DrumFord X does **not** host, distribute, or transmit any song audio. Audio
files are supplied by you (by importing) or are contained in the community
chart packages you choose to download.

## Children's privacy

DrumFord X is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone,
including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a
